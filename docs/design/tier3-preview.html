What we plan to build, for your approval before any code. Styles come from the app; layout follows the decisions from our review.

View as

The phone is clickable: tabs, search (try "data structures"), Sort, Filters, Save, My Books, Settings.

Announcements on the desk page

  • A new Announcements page beside the desk. Any Librarian can create, edit and end one; every change goes to the audit log. Q10
  • The poster image is required, the title is required (read by screen readers, not drawn on the banner), the body and the Book link are optional. Q20
  • The crop frame is fixed at 2:1 and shows exactly what Members see on Home. Drag it. The detail screen always shows the uncropped poster. Q21
  • Only one runs at a time: date ranges cannot overlap, so later ones queue as Scheduled. "End now" stops the running one early. Q22

Try it

  • Drag the yellow frame up and down; the Home preview on the right follows.
  • Upload one of your own pubmats with Replace image. It stays in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The dates start overlapping Library Week, so Schedule is blocked. Move the start to Nov 29 or later.
  • One optional link: a Book (View book) or a web page such as a sign-up form or a Facebook post. A web link must start with https://, its button names the site ("Open forms.gle"), and it opens in the Member's own browser, not inside LIBRAS.
